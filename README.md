### Business Problem Summary
Wanjiku operates a fresh-produce stall at Gikomba Market and needs data-driven clarity on her 14-day business performance. By analyzing daily sales fluctuations, weekday versus weekend trends, and current inventory stock levels, this project identifies growth patterns, revenue drivers, and critical restocking vulnerabilities to optimize profitability and prevent stockouts.

### Top Three Findings
1. **Strong Weekend Surge:** Weekend daily sales average KES 4,950, which is 42.15% higher than weekday averages (KES 3,480), highlighting weekends as Wanjiku's primary revenue driver.
2. **Positive Business Growth:** Week-on-week revenue grew by 15.68% from Week 1 (KES 22,500) to Week 2 (KES 26,030).
3. **Critical Inventory Risk:** Both Tomatoes (18 kg vs 25 kg reorder level) and Sukuma Wiki (9 kg vs 15 kg reorder level) are severely understocked and require immediate replenishment.
